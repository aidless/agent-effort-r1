`CLAIM_LEDGER.md` points at as evidence, copied verbatim. Nothing here was selected
by hand, so the bundle cannot drift from the claims.

relative positions so a ledger pointer of the form `results/x.json -> field` can be

## Absolute paths carried by these files (disclosed, not rewritten)

The artefacts below are shipped **byte-identical** to the versions the claims were
written against. Some of them record absolute paths from the machine that produced
them. Those paths were left untouched on purpose: editing evidence to look tidy would
make the released bytes differ from the bytes the numbers came from. They identify a
rented machine and an authoring directory; they carry no author identity beyond what
the paper already states.
