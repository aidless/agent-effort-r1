# R1 changes and material limitations

The earlier anonymous TMLR draft is preserved as main.before-arxiv-r1.tex. Current manuscript is a full descriptive rewrite, with real confirmed author metadata and AI-use disclosure. It removes source-length quotes, first-unified-cost assertions, independent-n=16 claims, inferred verification-collapse mechanisms, portable timing ratios, universal marginal-gain identification, and claims that E2 savings are empirically achieved. E3 is labeled an oracle schedule accounting control; H4 outcomes are recorded test-pass events.

Recomputed all latency means/counts/failure frequencies, all H4 condition coverage, task/menu/repeat dependence, task-level contrasts and leave-one-task-out ranges; independently rederived E2 algebra, matched minima and token frontiers. No model inference or network experiment was conducted.

Unresolved underlying defects remain: missing exact runtime source, missing complete prompts/responses/code/final programs, grading isolation limits, repeated starter/history truncation, eight authored test tasks with limited grading. Disclosure narrows the paper but does not repair the experiments or make them top-conference quality. Independent review decides whether the descriptive wording needs further changes.
